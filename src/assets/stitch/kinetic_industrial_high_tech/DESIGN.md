## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-precision, cinematic industrial engineering aesthetic. It unites heavy mechanical engineering rigor with cutting-edge digital showcase design, capturing the essence of advanced infrastructure, technical pipeline architecture, and high-spec industrial execution.

### Design Movement & Ethos
- **Technical Brutalism meets Precision Glassmorphism:** Monolithic structure, razor-sharp geometric discipline, and exposed technical metadata balanced by luminous, translucent surface panels and razor-thin luminous rules.
- **Architectural Drafting & Telemetry:** Delicate AutoCAD blueprint underlays, isometric dimension ticks, crosshair reticles, and live telemetry badges transform static web layouts into high-grade control consoles and cinematic product dossiers.
- **Atmosphere:** Deep carbon void, controlled photon emissions via safety neon accents, and meticulous CAD callouts.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Engineers, energy sector executives, procurement directors, and technical infrastructure clients. The UI projects unyielding operational integrity, mathematical precision, mission-critical dependability, and state-of-the-art technological capability.

## Colors

The palette leverages an ultra-deep void baseline, punctuated by active photon-emitting functional accents and architectural blueprint markers.

### Palette Architecture
- **Safety Orange (`#FF6A00` / `#FF7B1A`):** The primary kinetic signal. Reserved for critical CTAs, active telemetry indicators, execution triggers, and operational status warnings.
- **Engineer Blue (`#0284C7` / `#2563EB`):** The technical anchor. Applied to structural lines, schematic drafting grids, CAD measurement markers, and metadata linkages.
- **Steel Silver (`#94A3B8` / `#CBD5E1`):** Structural neutral typography and secondary technical readouts. Represents milled steel and structural framing.
- **Carbon & Midnight Graphite (`#050608` to `#181F2C`):** Multi-tiered, light-absorbent base surfaces that suppress glare and emphasize illuminated precision data.

### Contrast & Functional Roles
- Backgrounds remain predominantly low-luminance (`#050608` to `#0B0E14`).
- All active interactive states leverage high-contrast, glowing phosphors (`#FF6A00` with alpha-mask glow halos).
- Blueprint grid lines maintain a strict opacity floor of `0.06` to a maximum ceiling of `0.22` to prevent interference with tabular data and schematic labels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements three distinct type treatments to establish operational clarity:

1. **Space Grotesk (Display & Headlines):** Imparts an engineered, geometric profile with architectural cuts on stems and terminals. Used for large scale impact, metric readouts, and section landmarks.
2. **Inter (Body & Narrative):** Delivers clean readability for dense engineering documentation, technical specifications, and project narratives.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Technical Metadata & Telemetry):** Serves engineering specs, coordinates, pipeline diameters, registration IDs (`RC: 9665822`), ISO standards, and real-time operational parameters.

All monospace metadata should be set in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.05em` to `0.08em`) to mimic plotter outputs and instrument HUDs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a rigid 4px base cadence and an engineered 12-column architectural grid.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Blueprint Coordinate System:** Visual columns align to visible CAD grid marks (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08)`). Intersections host small `+` crosshair reticles at major container boundaries.
- **Dense vs. Cinematic Spacing:** High density within component interiors (telemetry stats, engineering tables) juxtaposed with spacious section rhythms (`96px` to `128px` vertical spacing) on macro showcases.

### Breakpoints & Reflow
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, max-width `1440px`, `32px` gutters, outer margin dynamic centered.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `24px` gutters, `32px` page margins. Technical sidebars collapse into toggleable telemetry drawers.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column grid, `16px` gutters, `16px` margins. Isometric schematic modules switch to 2D flat wireframe layers or horizontally scrollable data sheets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through luminosity, layered blueprint geometry, and industrial glass transparency rather than traditional soft drop shadows.

### Elevation Layers
1. **Layer 0 (Bedrock Canvas):** Pure carbon black `#050608` overlaid with a subtle 32px repeating isometric dot or grid pattern in `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.06)`.
2. **Layer 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** Solid graphite `#0B0E14` or translucent glass `rgba(11, 14, 20, 0.85)` with a `12px` backdrop blur and a `1px` structural hairline border of `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)`.
3. **Layer 2 (Active/Hover State):** Surface rises to `#121721` with blueprint boundary lines shifting to `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.45)`. Ambient corner brackets in safety orange illuminate via localized box shadows (`0 0 20px rgba(255, 106, 0, 0.15)`).
4. **Layer 3 (Modals, Overlays & HUD Telemetry):** `rgba(18, 23, 33, 0.95)` with double-layered borders: an inner `1px` stroke of `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.3)` and an outer faint glow halo `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.

### Dimensional Indicators
- **Corner Notches & Reticles:** Major containers employ 45-degree angled clipped corners or explicit corner brackets (`L`-shaped 6px ticks).
- **Precision Laser Rulers:** Active interactive dividers feature high-intensity gradient hairline dividers (`transparent -> #FF6A00 -> transparent` or `transparent -> #0284C7 -> transparent`).

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly architectural, industrial, and sharp (`roundedness: 0`). Curved, playful corners are prohibited; form reflects machined steel, cut glass, structural trussing, and CAD drafting discipline.

### Geometric Rules
- **Standard Corners:** All structural elements—buttons, cards, inputs, tags, and viewports—feature true `0px` radius corners.
- **Chamfered / Beveled Cuts:** Where visual interest or technical differentiation is needed, use a `6px` or `8px` CSS `clip-path` chamfer (45-degree diagonal cut) on top-right or opposing diagonal corners.
- **Reticle Details:** Critical system panels display micro corner brackets (2px wide, 6px long) simulating technical viewfinders and assembly jig fixtures.

## Components

### Buttons & Operational Triggers
- **Primary Industrial Trigger:** Background of solid `#FF6A00`, text in `#050608` (bold JetBrains Mono, uppercase), `0px` radius, padding `14px 28px`. Hover: background shifts to `#FF7B1A` with a laser glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 106, 0, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Blueprint Action:** Background transparent, `1px` solid border `#0284C7`, text `#F8FAFC`. Hover: fills with `rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)` and border brightens to `#38BDF8`.
- **Ghost Data Action:** Text `#94A3B8`, leading icon or telemetry bracket (`[ RUN_DIAGNOSTIC ]`). Hover: text turns `#FF6A00`.

### Technical Badges & Status Chips
- Monospace font (`label-code-sm`), uppercase.
- Encased in a `1px` hairline outline with a left-aligned status light indicator (4px glowing dot: `#FF6A00` for active, `#0284C7` for operational, `#22C55E` for calibrated).
- Example: `[ RC: 9665822 // SPEC-4 ]` rendered in `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.1)` fill with steel silver typography.

### Industrial Cards & Modules
- Base: `#0B0E14` with `1px` border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)`.
- Header: Technical panel label bar with top-right coordinate stamp (e.g., `SEC_04 // LOC: 6.5244° N`).
- Optional subtle CAD grid overlay mask on card backgrounds with lower-third isometric watermark.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- Background: `#07090D`, `1px` border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`.
- Sharp `0px` corners, height `48px`, font JetBrains Mono `14px`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#FF6A00` with subtle internal drop glow. Accompanying input labels include an index prefix (e.g., `01 // PROJECT_SPEC`).

### High-Spec Stats Counter & Spec Panels
- Stat metric set in `stat-metric` (Space Grotesk, 56px, bold), with a high-contrast safety orange or electric blue decimal point/accent.
- Sub-labels rendered in JetBrains Mono (`label-code-xs`), paired with an animated micro-bar gauge or pipeline flow vector line beneath the count.

### Isometric Schematics & Dimension Rules
- Schematics are framed by blueprint dimension lines with dual-arrow terminals and live dimension labels (e.g., `◄── 1,240 MM ──►`).
- Critical node points on schematics feature pulsing concentric ring markers in `#0284C7` linked to floating frosted-glass tooltips.
