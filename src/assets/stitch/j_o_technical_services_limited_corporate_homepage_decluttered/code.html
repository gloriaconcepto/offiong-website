Corporate Governance & Profile

Rooted in Engineering Rigor and Regional Commitment

J&O Technical Services Limited is an indigenous oilfield service and technical engineering contractor registered under Nigerian law (RC: 9665822). Operating from our integrated yard in Port Harcourt, we provide agile and high-precision support to upstream operators, international oil companies (IOCs), and national infrastructure bodies.

Our culture is driven by uncompromising statutory compliance, local talent empowerment under the NOGICD Act, and an unblemished record of safe execution across sensitive riverine and swamp environments.
