## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, premier-tier corporate identity tailored for world-class technical engineering, LNG infrastructure, and high-scale energy logistics. It merges the institutional gravitas and sustainability narrative of sovereign energy enterprises (inspired by Nigeria LNG) with the high-precision operational clarity of industrial technical services.

The design movement is **Corporate Modern with High-Precision Technical Accents**:
- **Authoritative & Institutional**: Expansive editorial layouts, rigorous grid alignment, and prominent operational metrics convey industrial mastery and geopolitical reliability.
- **Modern Energy & Sustainability**: Rich deep slate and maritime navy foundations are balanced with vibrant Nigerian energy greens and high-visibility industrial orange accents.
- **Editorial Fluidity & Tactile Rigor**: Structured stats cards, rounded visual reels, and technical metadata labels evoke both board-level executive poise and field-tested engineering safety.

## Colors

The palette synthesizes sovereign energy leadership and heavy engineering rigor:
- **Primary (`#006A4E` - Energy Emerald)**: Anchors brand trust, environmental responsibility, and Nigerian national resource stewardship. Used for primary interactive actions, executive category badges, and active operational highlights.
- **Secondary (`#0B192C` - Slate Midnight)**: Represents institutional permanence, maritime deepwater infrastructure, and corporate precision. Applied to hero sections, heavy stats blocks, text hierarchy, and executive footers.
- **Tertiary (`#FF6A00` - Safety Industrial Orange)**: Sourced from engineering safety emblems and technical field operations. Directs eye tracking toward critical interactive conversions, emergency notices, field technical badges, and directional arrows.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` - Slate Steel)**: Governs body typography, technical subheaders, and subtle divider systems, ensuring maximum readability without harsh pitch-black contrast.

Backgrounds leverage a dual-tone approach: crisp `#FFFFFF` for data-heavy executive tables and technical cards, layered over warm `#F9FAF6` and cool off-white `#F1F5F9` sections to structure long-scroll corporate landing architectures.

## Typography

The pairing combines the engineering punch of **Space Grotesk** with the neutral, hyper-legible utility of **Inter**:
- **Space Grotesk (Headlines & Labels)**: Carries mathematical precision, structured terminal angles, and subtle monospaced mechanical cues. Used across display heroes, section titles, quantitative statistics, and operational pill badges.
- **Inter (Body & Meta Text)**: Resolves long-form technical prose, sustainability reports, and navigational menus with zero ocular fatigue and universal cross-platform clarity.

All uppercase labels utilize wide tracking (`0.08em`) to mimic engineering blueprints and industrial control schematics.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict **12-column responsive fluid grid** with a locked max-width container of `1360px`:
- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `3rem` (48px) container padding.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, `1.25rem` (20px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1rem` (16px) screen edge margins.

Section vertical rhythms strictly adhere to `space-3xl` (72px) and `space-4xl` (96px) on desktop to establish an unhurried, monumental editorial pace. Asymmetrical bento-style stats grids and media reels dynamically span 4, 6, 8, or 12 columns with standardized internal card paddings (`space-xl` / 32px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines crisp architectural layering and soft ambient enterprise shadows:
- **Level 0 (Flat / Technical Base)**: Pure background surfaces (`#FFFFFF` or `#F9FAF6`) bounded by hairline border dividers (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Used for table matrices and operational dashboards.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest)**: Subtle elevation for executive cards and operational tiles using `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Operational Cards)**: Elevated state for clickable services, reports, and interactive reels using `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.10)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Sticky Nav)**: Frosted structural glass for navigational ribbons using `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and `box-shadow: 0 8px 30px rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.08)`.
- **Industrial Glow Accent**: Focus rings and primary interactions utilize an energetic ambient glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 106, 0, 0.25)` or `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 106, 78, 0.20)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape strategy to emulate contemporary engineering precision without stark brutalist aggression:
- Standard interactive elements (input fields, buttons, small chips) inherit `0.5rem` (8px) radius.
- Cards, operational reels, and imagery feature `1rem` (16px) radius (`rounded-lg`).
- Feature showcase panels, high-impact hero bento cards, and interactive round-corner frames leverage `1.5rem` (24px) radius (`rounded-xl`).
- Category pills, ticker notifications, and circular pagination arrows feature full `9999px` pill contours.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Corporate Action**: Background `#006A4E`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, font `Space Grotesk` 600, padding `12px 24px`, radius `8px`. Hover: `#004F3A` with smooth transform `translateY(-1px)`.
- **Tertiary Industrial / Safety Action**: Background `#FF6A00`, foreground `#FFFFFF`. Used for "Engage Service", "Urgent Announcements", or critical tender notices. Hover: `#E05D00`.
- **Ghost / Outlined**: Border `1px solid #0B192C`, text `#0B192C`, transparent background. Hover: fills with `#0B192C`, text becomes `#FFFFFF`.
- **Directional Navigation Controls**: Circular `44px x 44px` pill buttons with centered chevron icons in `#003A0F` or `#FF6A00` with subtle border and crisp active push feedback.

### Chips & Operational Pills
- **Section Eyebrows**: Pill contour (`rounded-full`), padding `6px 14px`, text `label-technical` (uppercase, tracking `0.08em`).
- **Sustainability Pill**: Background `#F0F8C2`, text `#003A0F`.
- **Safety / Field Service Pill**: Background `#FFF3EB`, text `#FF6A00`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 106, 0, 0.2)`.

### Cards & Media Reels
- **Stats Card**: Deep slate navy background (`#0B192C`) or high-contrast white. Large `Space Grotesk` numerical displays (`stats-numeral`) accompanied by muted supporting labels and subtle translucent watermarked grid accents.
- **Operations & News Reel**: Asymmetric vertical-oriented cards with full-bleed imagery, rounded `16px` corners, subtle dark bottom gradient overlay (`rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.85)`), crisp white headline, and circular floating action indicator.
- **Executive & Capability Profile**: Clean white card on off-white canvas, top border accentuated by a 3px hairline brand green or orange stroke.

### Input Fields & Search
- Border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0B192C`, radius `8px`, height `48px`. Focus: border color `#006A4E`, outline ring `3px rgba(0, 106, 78, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `20px x 20px`, border `2px solid #94A3B8`, active fill `#006A4E` with crisp white checkmark SVG. Focus ring in safety industrial orange.

### Corporate Footer & Notice Modal
- **Footer**: Monolithic deep slate `#0B192C` surface with crisp off-white typography, multi-column navigation, and safety certifications.
- **Compliance & Notice Modal**: Centered floating card with a high-visibility header banner, safety-orange cautionary icon, and structured dual-action dismissal buttons.
