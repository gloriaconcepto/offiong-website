The 10 Golden Safety Rules

Engineered to prevent high-potential incidents in oil and gas environments. Compliance is non-negotiable for all employees, sub-contractors, and visiting inspectors.

01 assignment_turned_in

Permit to Work

Never initiate high-risk, hot work, or critical swamp fabrication without a fully validated PTW signed by the area authority.

Mandatory Cold & Hot Work
02 meeting_room

Confined Space

Confirm gas atmosphere tests, atmospheric ventilation, continuous standby sentry, and certified rescue harnesses prior to entry.

Tanks, Pits & Vessels
03 height

Working at Height

100% tie-off using certified full-body harnesses, double lanyards, and inspected scaffolding above 1.8 meters.

1.8m Elevation Standard
04 lock

Energy Isolation

Strict Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) for electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, and pressurized manifold systems prior to maintenance.

LOTO Protocols
05 air

Gas Testing

Continuous multi-gas atmospheric monitoring for hydrocarbon vapors, LEL, oxygen levels, and toxic H2S presence.

LEL & H2S Continuous Test
06 hard_drive

PPE Compliance

Mandatory flame-retardant coveralls, hard hats, steel-toe ballistic boots, ballistic safety glasses, and ear defense.

100% Mandatory Gear
07 precision_manufacturing

Lifting Operations

No personnel underneath suspended loads. Certified rigging equipment, certified crane operators, and verified lift plans.

Barricaded Lift Radius
08 local_fire_department

Hot Work Control

Combustible material shielding, fire watch standing by with pressurized foam extinguishers for 60 minutes post-weld.

Spark Containment
09 rv_hookup

Line of Fire

Position yourself clear of heavy machinery swing radiuses, high-pressure test manifolds, and moving barge cables.

Hazard Exclusion Zones
10 directions_boat

Swamp & Water Safety

Mandatory lifejackets, certified water survival training, and emergency skiff standby across riverine operations.
